from metadata setup and cannot be cleared by catalog access, account discovery,
settings, delivery alarms, or reading a discrepancy report.

selected protected credential's owner/hash proof, exact Root/credential epochs,
and the active imported generation. Readiness carried in an archive is not proof.

invalidation can mark only never-attempted prepared intents for rebuilding and
clear their stale authorization; UUID, payload and attempt evidence are preserved.

settings operation can finish its own durable hold after freshness expires;
credential, generation and mirror proof must still match.
